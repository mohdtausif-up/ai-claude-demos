Product Requirements Document: Invite.com

Product category: Online invitations, RSVP management, attendance polling and real-time guest tracking
 Proposed technology: PHP 8.1, MySQL 8, JavaScript, HTML5 and modern CSS
 Primary channels: Responsive web application, Progressive Web App, email, SMS and WhatsApp sharing
 Document status: Product concept and implementation-ready PRD
 Working product name: Invite.com

Important naming note: Before development or branding investment, verify ownership and trademark availability for Invite.com. The domain may already be registered even if it does not appear prominently in search results. A fallback brand and domain should be prepared.

1. Executive Summary

Invite.com will be a modern invitation and event-management platform where individuals, families, event planners and businesses can:

Create a beautiful digital invitation.
Add event details, schedules, maps, media and RSVP questions.
Invite guests using a public link, private personalized link, QR code, email, SMS or WhatsApp.
Let guests respond with Attending, Not Attending or Maybe without creating an account.
Track responses, headcount, plus-ones, meal choices and pending invitations in real time.
Send reminders and updates to selected guest segments.
Check guests in using QR codes.
Produce reports for hosts, caterers, venues and event planners.
Manage multi-day and multi-function events from one dashboard.

The product should combine the simplicity and social experience of Partiful, the invitation designs of Greetings Island and Evite, the advanced guest management of RSVPify, and the multi-function event capabilities of Invyt. Partiful currently provides interactive invitations, RSVP tracking, date polling, guest questions, shared albums and group updates, while RSVPify extends the model with guest segmentation, sub-events, payments, seating and event communication.

The competitive opportunity is to place these capabilities in one fast, mobile-first product with better privacy, multilingual support, India-friendly sharing, accessible design, transparent pricing and significantly stronger reporting.

4. Goals and Non-Goals
4.1 Product goals
Primary goals
Allow a host to publish an invitation in under two minutes.
Allow a guest to RSVP in under 20 seconds.
Show updated RSVP information to the host within two seconds.
Reduce the need for manual spreadsheets and individual follow-ups.
Support casual events, weddings, corporate events and community gatherings.
Provide an elegant experience on low-end mobile devices and slower networks.
Support both public-link events and strictly controlled private events.
Establish Invite.com as a privacy-conscious alternative to ad-heavy invitation products.
Business goals
Convert free users through premium designs and advanced management features.
Monetize through event-based plans and optional subscriptions.
Create a planner and agency tier with white-label capabilities.
Support high-margin add-ons such as SMS credits, custom domains and AI design generation.
Encourage organic acquisition through shared invitation pages, without intrusive branding.
4.2 Non-goals for initial release

The first release will not attempt to provide:

Full venue-management software
Full hotel-room reservation infrastructure
A general social network
Video-conferencing infrastructure
Complex professional conference scheduling
Native iOS and Android apps
Large-scale event ticket marketplaces
Full accounting or enterprise-resource planning

These can be integrated or added after core RSVP workflows are stable.

19. Acceptance Criteria for MVP

The MVP is ready when:

A new host can create and publish an invitation without assistance.
A guest can RSVP on mobile without an account.
The host sees the response in the dashboard within the defined update interval.
Guests can change their responses using a secure link.
The host can distinguish adults, children and plus-ones.
The host can filter and export guest data.
Pending guests can receive reminders.
Private invitations cannot be viewed without a valid token or configured verification.
Invitation pages meet core WCAG 2.2 AA checks.
Duplicate RSVP submissions do not create duplicate records.
All sensitive actions are protected by authorization and audit logging.
Public pages meet agreed performance targets.
Email bounce and delivery failures are visible to hosts.
Event deletion follows the documented retention policy.
Core workflows work on desktop, Android and iPhone browsers.
20. Recommended Product Positioning
Primary headline

Beautiful invitations. Instant RSVPs. Complete event clarity.

Supporting copy

Create and share a modern digital invitation in minutes. Guests can respond without an account, while you track attendance, plus-ones, preferences and check-ins in real time.

Core differentiator

Invite.com should not present itself only as an invitation-card maker. Its strongest positioning is:

An invitation experience for guests and a real-time event command center for hosts.

That positioning supports both simple birthdays and complex weddings or corporate events without making the product feel like enterprise software.

Final Recommendation

Start with a carefully constrained MVP centered on three exceptional experiences:

A beautiful invitation builder
A nearly frictionless guest RSVP
A genuinely useful live host dashboard

Do not initially overload the application with seating, budgeting, ticketing and AI. Design the data model to support those modules, but first make invitation creation, mobile sharing, attendance collection and live reporting excellent. The main competitive advantage will come from combining Partiful-level simplicity, RSVPify-level control, WhatsApp-first distribution, regional event support and privacy-by-default guest management in one coherent interface.
